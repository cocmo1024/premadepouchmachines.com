What the symptom can indicate

01

Pouch lips may be blocked by static, zipper compression, surface treatment or dimensional variation.

02

A pickup problem can look like an opening problem after the pouch reaches the station out of position.

03

Vacuum level at the pump does not prove vacuum flow and seal condition at the suction cup.

Buyer analysis

Make the machine safe before touching the opening mechanism

Stop the machine and follow the site's approved energy-isolation procedure before inspecting, cleaning, repositioning or replacing suction cups, hoses, grippers, guides or sensors. Release or secure stored pneumatic and mechanical energy as required by the machine documentation. Never reach through guarding, defeat an interlock or make a hands-on adjustment while the mechanism can move; motion should be observed from outside the guarded area or through an approved diagnostic mode.

Use the first deviation as the root of the fault tree

Classify each failure by the earliest incorrect event: no pouch leaves the magazine; the pouch is picked but handed off skewed; the pouch reaches the opener but neither lip separates; only one side separates; the mouth opens and then collapses; or the machine reports open when the mouth is not usable. Diagnose that first deviation rather than the later jam, empty fill or seal reject it creates.

Build a pouch-lot evidence set before changing settings

Retain identified samples from a previously stable lot and the failed lot. With the pouches off the machine, compare mouth width, lip offset, zipper location, top-edge cut, curl, blocking, surface feel and the pattern of hand-opening resistance. Sample different positions in the stack and preserve the orientation of every failed pouch so a recurring face, edge or zipper condition is visible.

Measure vacuum as a path, not as one gauge value

Install any measurement connection only while the machine is stopped and isolated, then observe the instrument from a safe location during an approved test cycle. Compare the source, distribution point and opening cup at the moment opening is commanded. A normal source reading with a weak or delayed cup reading directs the search toward restrictions, leaks, valve response, hose movement or poor cup contact instead of toward the pump setting.

Separate pouch contact from pickup and gripper geometry

After isolation, inspect the cup face for wear, contamination and an incomplete contact mark. Check that the pouch is centered and supported when the cup lands, and that grippers hold it level without covering the opening zone or distorting the zipper. A pouch that arrives late, low or skewed should be corrected at pickup or handoff before vacuum or opening dwell is changed.

Run challenge samples that identify the weak boundary

Use identified retained-good and suspect-lot pouches, plus approved production samples representing stack position, normal curl, zipper compression and dimensional variation. Run each group separately and record the first deviation, not only the final reject. Include controlled no-pouch, unopened-pouch and partially opened-pouch conditions to verify detection, product-inhibit logic, reject handling and recovery without placing hands inside the guarded process.

Change one cause boundary and preserve the baseline

Choose the next adjustment from the evidence: pouch presentation, cup contact, a confirmed vacuum-path loss, gripper position, opening timing or detection logic. Record the original setting, the reason for the change and the outcome by pouch lot. Do not increase vacuum or dwell simply because a failed pouch remained closed; excessive force can deform the pouch, accelerate cup wear or hide an upstream handoff error.

Define stop conditions before the trial

Stop the test if a pouch or mechanism can strike a gripper, jaw, nozzle or guard; if motion becomes erratic; if a hose, cup, guide or fastener is loose; if stored energy cannot be controlled; if guarding or an interlock is unavailable; or if the planned observation would expose a person to moving parts. Do not continue cycling to collect more data after a collision risk or safety-control fault appears.

Release the correction only after variation testing

Repeat the test with the approved pouch lots, normal magazine levels and the production recipe. Confirm reliable pickup, full opening, valid opening detection, product inhibit on failed openings, controlled reject recovery and finished-pack quality. Record good output and failure classification over a representative run; a single successful cycle does not establish a stable correction.

Common decision questions

Why can the supply vacuum gauge look normal when the pouch does not open?

The supply gauge shows only one point in the system. A restricted filter, delayed valve, cracked or moving hose, worn cup, poor cup contact or short opening command can leave the cup with insufficient flow or response during the actual event. Measure at defined points and at the event before changing the source setting.

Should vacuum be increased first?

No. First confirm that the pouch arrives centered, the cup seals on the intended surface and the dynamic vacuum reaches the cup at the correct time. Increasing vacuum without that evidence can distort the pouch, shorten cup life or mask a transfer error.

How do we separate a pouch-lot problem from a machine problem?

Run identified retained-good and suspect-lot samples without mixing them, preserve failed orientations and compare mouth geometry, curl, blocking, zipper location and hand-opening pattern. If the failure follows the lot under the same controlled setup, investigate the material specification and converting process before retuning the machine.

What proves the fault is upstream of the opening station?

A pouch that is late, low, skewed or inconsistently gripped has already departed from the intended sequence before opening begins. Marking the first visible deviation with safe external video prevents a downstream symptom from being treated as the root cause.

Can the cups or grippers be adjusted while the machine is cycling?

No hands-on inspection or adjustment should be made while the mechanism can move. Stop the machine, follow the site's approved energy-isolation procedure and control stored energy before touching those parts. Observe cycles only from a guarded safe position or with an approved diagnostic procedure.

Which challenge conditions should the opening check include?

Use retained-good and suspect lots plus approved production variation such as stack position, normal curl, zipper compression and dimensional spread. Also verify the safe machine response to no pouch, unopened pouch and partial opening, including product inhibit, reject handling and recovery.

When is the correction ready to become the standard recipe?

Only after it repeats across approved pouch lots and normal magazine variation while maintaining full opening, correct detection, failed-opening product inhibit, reject recovery and finished-pack quality. One successful pouch or a short unloaded run is not adequate evidence.

What the symptom can indicate

01

Teams often time only mechanical adjustment and omit cleaning, line clearance and first-good-pack verification.

02

Unlabeled change parts and uncontrolled settings create repeated searching and correction.

03

A faster changeover that increases startup scrap does not improve effective capacity.

Buyer analysis

Define start and finish boundaries

Measure from last good pack of the old SKU to first verified good pack of the new SKU. Include cleaning, material removal, coding, quality release and documentation.

Move preparation outside the stopped window

Pre-stage verified film, pouches, parts, tools, recipes and labels. External preparation should be controlled so the speed gain does not introduce wrong-component risk.

Standardize the first-good-pack check

Use fixed samples and criteria for weight, seal, code, registration and appearance. Record adjustments so repeated SKUs start closer to the validated recipe.

RFQ checklist for this page

A short video showing the complete machine cycle before, during and after the fault.

Photos of good and failed packs with the defect location marked.

Current recipe, setpoints, alarms, line speed and dynamic air or vacuum readings where relevant.

Product, pouch or film samples from both good and failed lots.

Recent change parts, maintenance actions, material changes and the measured reject rate.

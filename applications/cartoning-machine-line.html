Buyer analysis

The product feeder defines the real project

Carton forming is only one function. Product orientation, leaflet insertion, counting and transfer from primary packaging determine line complexity and output.

Challenge carton variation

Board stiffness, glue, tuck geometry and storage humidity affect opening and closing. Acceptance needs cartons from production lots, not hand-selected samples.

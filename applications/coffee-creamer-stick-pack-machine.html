Buyer analysis

Powder composition affects flow and residue

Fat content, particle size and static can change hopper flow and contamination around the seal. Send each commercial formula for testing.

Barrier and seal quality protect shelf life

Final laminate, seal width, easy-open requirement and carton configuration should be validated with the target distribution period.

What the project must solve

01

A first inquiry does not need a finished engineering specification. Identify the product, package, destination country and contact, and mark unknown details rather than delaying contact.

02

Suppliers cannot compare output or filling accuracy when the RFQ omits the SKU, commercial packaging material, measurement method or operating conditions.

03

A machine-only quote may exclude feeding, inspection, change parts and downstream handling. Define who supplies each interface before comparing totals.

Buyer analysis

Separate must-have acceptance criteria from preferences

State product, package, output, accuracy, seal and compliance requirements as measurable obligations. List optional features separately so quotations remain comparable.

Attach evidence, not only descriptions

Product, film, pouch, carton and finished-pack samples reveal geometry and behavior that a written RFQ misses. Include videos of upstream and downstream processes.

Use a two-stage RFQ instead of making first contact difficult

The first message can contain contact details, country, product and package photos, with unknowns clearly marked. Use the engineering follow-up to freeze SKU dimensions, fill range, accepted-output targets, materials and interfaces. Photos support the initial discussion; commercial product and packaging samples may still be needed for fit and performance commitments. Agree any physical sample shipment separately.

Make one SKU worksheet and one scope schedule

For each SKU record product, fill target, package drawing, material structure, batch size and required accepted packs per shift. For each module record included, optional, buyer-supplied or excluded, plus the interface owner. Record utility conditions, cleaning constraints and inspection requirements alongside the relevant module. This separates a comparable proposal from a list of unrelated machine features.

Turn missing information into a written action list

Ask the supplier to identify assumptions, compatibility risks, sample checks and documents needed for the next decision. Assign unresolved product, material or interface questions to an owner. A budgetary quotation should stay labeled budgetary until those assumptions are reviewed. Keep confidential production data in the direct inquiry rather than adding it to public website content.

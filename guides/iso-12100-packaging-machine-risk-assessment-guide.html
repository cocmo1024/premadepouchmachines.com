Buyer analysis

Risk assessment should follow the machine lifecycle

Review intended use, foreseeable misuse, operation, cleaning, changeover, jam clearing, maintenance, transport and decommissioning rather than only normal production.

Ask for evidence, not a generic compliance sentence

The RFQ can request the risk-assessment method, identified residual risks, protective measures, validation records and safety information included with the machine.

Sources and standards

ISO 12100:2010

ISO describes the current published standard as a methodology for machinery risk assessment and risk reduction; ISO also shows a revision is under development.

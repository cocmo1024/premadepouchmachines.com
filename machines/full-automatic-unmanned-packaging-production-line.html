Machine overview

An end-of-line integration guide based on the brochure's case opening, packing, sealing and conveying scope. Primary packaging and inspection remain separate project interfaces. The line should be specified around the incoming pack orientation, case pattern, rate, case quality and recovery from missing or misaligned products.

Acceptance checks before order

01

Validate the target accepted output with real product samples, final package format dimensions and the chosen dosing method.

02

Run seal and leak checks with the final package format material, filling temperature and expected product residue.

03

Confirm the selected dosing method accuracy, cleaning access, product-contact material and changeover time before the final quotation.

Buyer checklist before quotation

Target products: Finished retail cartons, Grouped products for case packing, Shipping cases, Logistics-ready secondary packs.

Target package: retail carton, grouped product, shipping case.

Options to confirm: Case erector, Case packing station, Case sealer, Labeling interface, Conveyor controls, Palletizing interface.

Send product photos, samples, target output, voltage, compressed air and available footprint before final quotation.

  • Product name, photos, density, particle size, viscosity or piece dimensions.
  • Target fill weight or volume, accuracy requirement and output target.
  • Package dimensions, packaging material, pouch sample, container or carton size.
  • Required add-ons such as coding, nitrogen, vacuum, dust removal, capping, labeling, cartoning or case sealing.
  • Factory voltage, compressed air, local safety expectations, line layout and available footprint.
